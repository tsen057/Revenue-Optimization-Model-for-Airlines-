# Revenue Optimization Model for Airlines

This project focuses on building a data-driven **revenue optimization model** tailored for the **airline industry**, with the goal of maximizing profitability through intelligent pricing strategies and demand forecasting. By leveraging advanced statistical techniques and real-world aviation datasets, the model aims to support dynamic pricing and route management decisions.

## Project Objective

To develop a robust model that:
- Forecasts passenger demand based on market and seasonal trends.
- Optimizes pricing strategies to respond to changing demand and market conditions.
- Supports airline route and seat allocation decisions for better commercial performance.

## Key Features

-  **Demand Forecasting**: Utilized time-series and regression-based models to estimate passenger demand across different routes and time periods.
-  **Dynamic Pricing Strategy**: Integrated price elasticity modeling and revenue management principles to recommend optimal fare prices.
-  **Route Management Support**: Analyzed profitability across various routes using demand and pricing data.
-  **Data Storytelling**: Communicated insights clearly through visualizations and dashboards to inform key commercial decisions.

## Tech Stack & Tools

- **Languages**: Python
- **Data Visualization**: Power BI
- **Version Control**: GitHub


## Sample Insights

- Certain routes exhibit predictable seasonal demand spikes—price adjustments during peak seasons can boost yield by 8-10%.
- Price elasticity varies significantly by customer segment—suggesting tailored pricing can enhance competitiveness.
- Low-demand routes were identified for strategic review or promotional efforts.
